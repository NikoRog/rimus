Rimus prompt templates - v0.6.2

Core rule:
- The highlighted rhyme word is the final meaningful word in the sentence.
- The player rhymes with that final highlighted word.

Template variables:
- {ref} = the topic node, for example Grammar, Cinema, Biceps, Tennis.
- {target} = the highlighted final rhyme word.
- {person} = person / sentence subject.
- {object} = object / thing.
- {place} = place / setting.
- {action} = verb / action.
- {mood} = mood / adjective.
- {adverb} = how something happens.

Place / setting target templates:
- {person} found the {object} near the {target}.
- {person} had to {action} {adverb} across the {target}.
- {ref} started when {person} entered the {target}.
- {person} dropped the {object} beside the {target}.
- The whole {ref} scene moved toward the {target}.
- {person} tried to {action} before reaching the {target}.
- {ref} made everyone look at the {target}.
- {person} kept the {object} hidden at the {target}.

Person / subject target templates:
- The {object} was handed to the {target}.
- {ref} changed when {person} followed the {target}.
- {person} waited for the {target}.
- The {place} went quiet around the {target}.
- {person} could not ignore the {target}.
- {ref} gave the next turn to the {target}.
- {adverb}, the {object} pointed back to the {target}.
- The final clue belonged to the {target}.

Object / thing target templates:
- {person} tried to {action} with the {target}.
- {ref} became clear after finding the {target}.
- {person} passed around the {target}.
- The {place} was built around the {target}.
- {person} talked {adverb} about the {target}.
- The next rhyme came from the {target}.
- {person} left a mark on the {target}.
- {person} stared at the {target}.

Verb / action target templates:
- {person} had no choice but to {target}.
- {person} in {ref} wanted to {target}.
- The {object} made {person} try to {target}.
- At the {place}, the plan was to {target}.
- {person} moved {adverb} and started to {target}.
- The crowd shouted for {person} to {target}.
- The round changed when {person} chose to {target}.
- {ref} made it hard to {target}.

Mood / adjective target templates:
- The {place} suddenly felt {target}.
- {person} made the whole room feel {target}.
- The {object} turned the moment {target}.
- {ref} became strangely {target}.
- {person} grew more {target}.
- The next rhyme sounded {target}.
- {person} went quiet and {target}.
- That {ref} moment landed {target}.

Adverb / how target templates:
- {person} used the {object} {target}.
- {person} watched the room move {target}.
- The {place} changed when {person} acted {target}.
- {ref} made the whole group react {target}.
- The next line had to land {target}.
- {person} tried to {action} {target}.

Knowledge system:
- 14 subjects.
- 6 domain/knowledge nodes per subject.
- 150-200 curated items per subject target range.
- Current packs contain role vocab, definitions, facts, and subject templates inside each node.
- Each subject has its own file in `src/content/subjects/`.
- Each subject file contains nodes with id, topic, scene, people, places, objects, actions, moods, adverbs, definitions, facts, and role-specific templates.
- Each node pulls from subject-specific word pools for places, people, objects, actions, moods, and adverbs.
- Definitions explain the highlighted target word in that subject.
- Facts add a short learning note without slowing the game down.
- Subject-specific templates are used first; generic templates are fallback only.
- The prompt generator rejects lines where the target is not the final meaningful word or appears more than once.

Custom words:
- Custom words can be place/setting, sentence subject/person, object/thing, verb/action, mood/adjective, or adverb/how.
- Custom words can appear in any subject.
- Non-target placeholders have about a 36% chance to use a matching custom word.
- A sentence can use multiple custom words if their types match available slots.
- The final highlighted target still stays the rhyme word.
